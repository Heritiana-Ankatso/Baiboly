## Baiboly 1.2.0

**Install:** download the APK below and open it on the phone (allow installing from this source when asked).
Updates install over the app and keep your lists, notes, highlights and settings.

* Four themes, each a complete design: **Manuscript**, **Modern**, **Page** (like an e-reader, everything in one menu) and **Stained glass**; plus colour variants, typeface, titles and line spacing (Settings › Appearance).
* Three modes, one tap on the badge at the top left: **Casual** for everyday reading, **Church** (silent, screen kept on, previous / next passage of your service list), **Study** (original words, cross-references, dictionary, map).
* Simpler navigation: tabs at the bottom, the chapter title opens the books, the gear opens Settings, standard icons throughout.
* A new look: vellum and ink by day, warm black and gold by night; theme and language (MG, FR, EN) in Settings.
* Literal Standard Version (English).
* Cross-references for every verse; Bible dictionary, topics and the French Martin glossary.
* Offline map of Bible places, readable at every zoom (villages appear as you zoom in), with a timeline of nine periods and the borders of their time, roads (Old Testament highways, Roman roads), shaded relief and today's borders.
* Reading aloud, also with the screen off (switch it on in Settings); each text is read only with a voice in its own language.
* Catholic Bibles (73 books): Crampon 1923, néo-Crampon Libre, World English Bible Catholic Edition, Douay-Rheims. Turn them on in Settings › Advanced › Tradition.
* Study symbol: א for the Old Testament, α for the New.

**Note for users of earlier test builds:** those were signed with a temporary key. To move to this release once: open *Fitahirizana* (Backup) and export a backup, uninstall the old app, install this one, then restore the backup. From this release on, updates install directly.
