## Baiboly 1.7.0

**Install:** update from Settings › Advanced › Updates › *Download and install*, or download the APK below. Your lists, notes, highlights and settings are kept.

* **Quick look:** select a verse or a passage and a small card appears above the actions: the passage in another Bible (one in the interface language, or the one you choose; never the one you are reading) and the units it names, converted. Settings › Advanced › *Quick look* chooses what it shows: another Bible, units, people and events, places, the articles of your confessions that cite it. A tap opens the full Study panel there.
* **Timeline as a chart to scale** (Dictionary page, *Timeline*), from Creation (4004 BC) to the early church, after F. N. Jones's chronology:
  * twelve eras shade the background, their names kept at the top while they last;
  * lifetimes, reigns, judgeships and other periods are bars side by side, so overlaps show (Noah's life overlaps Abraham's; the kings of Israel and Judah reign in parallel);
  * other events are dots on the axis; labels appear as you zoom (pinch or double-tap), the most-told first, never overlapping;
  * *This chapter* picks out the events of the chapter you are reading; an era chip frames that era; a tap opens the event. *List* keeps the searchable list.
* **Confessions and councils:**
  * Catholic documents (the Canons and Decrees of Trent, the Profession of the Tridentine Faith, *Ineffabilis Deus*, the Vatican Council's decrees) and the Longer Catechism of the Orthodox Church, all 610 questions, with the Definition of Chalcedon: English of Philip Schaff, *The Creeds of Christendom* (1877), with their Scripture references as proof texts.
  * The seven ecumenical councils, Trent and the Vatican Council join Dort, Westminster and Savoy, each with what it decided and the traditions that receive it (Nicaea II: the Orthodox and Catholic Churches).
  * Your tradition (Settings › Advanced) shows its own documents and councils first; *Go deeper* the others, each marked, and only when there are some.
* **Settings** open in groups (Appearance, Default Bible, Reading aloud, Backup, About, Advanced), each on its own page, in every theme, with the theme and language at the top. *Interface size* no longer runs to the screen edges.
* **Map:** *Regions* can be found by name, listed A–Z with letter headings or by most named, and narrowed to lands and peoples, mountains and valleys, or seas, rivers and islands.
* **References in the interface language:** *John 3:16* in English, *Jn 3:16* in French, *Jao 3: 16* in Malagasy. Acts is *Asa* in Malagasy (*Asan* still works when typing).
* **Units:** metric only (mm, cm, m, km; mg, g, kg; mL, L). Wages are also given as a length of work: a talent, 6,000 days' wages, is about 19 years and 2 months of work at six days a week (Ex 20:9).
* **People:** God is no longer shown as a parent (Luke 3:38 calls Adam "the son of God" by creation, Gen 2:7); Joseph is the legal father of Jesus (Luke 3:23), and a note gives the virgin birth (Matt 1:18–25).
* **Fixes:** even left and right margins on Android 15 (justified text no longer carries letter spacing); an empty list in use is no longer shown under *Lists*, where Delete could not remove it.

## Baiboly 1.6.0

**Install:** download the APK below and open it on the phone, or update from Settings › Advanced › Updates › *Download and install*. Your lists, notes, highlights and settings are kept.

* **People of the Bible** (Study mode, Dictionary page, *People*): 3,068 people with their father, mother, spouses, brothers and sisters and children, the father line upwards, the events they take part in and every verse that names them. Biblical genealogies can skip generations, and the page says so. Names are in English (KJV). Data: Theographic Bible Metadata, CC BY-SA 4.0.
* **Timeline** (*Timeline*): 501 events from Creation to Acts. Dates follow F. N. Jones, *The Chronology of the Old Testament*; where Ussher's year (in the margin of the Treasury of Scripture Knowledge) differs, it is shown too. Each date is labelled as a reconstruction: Scripture gives ages and reigns, not dates.
* **Confessions** (*Confessions*): the Apostles', Nicene and Athanasian Creeds; the Belgic Confession, Heidelberg Catechism and Canons of Dort; the Westminster Confession, Larger and Shorter Catechisms; the French Confession, the Savoy Declaration and the 1689 Baptist Confession.
  * Historic English texts, in the public domain, with their proof texts (a tap opens the verse), cited as `WCF 1.2` or `HC Q&A 23`.
  * The councils and assemblies that issued them: Constantinople (381), Dort, Westminster, Savoy.
  * The **Tradition** setting decides what is shown: *Protestant* shows the creeds and the Reformed documents; *Catholic* shows the creeds, and the Reformed documents only under *Go deeper: other traditions*, each marked as such.
  * Not included yet: Catholic and Orthodox documents, other Reformed texts known only in modern translations under copyright (Second Helvetic Confession, Chalcedonian Definition), and Malagasy or French texts (none with a free licence was found).
* **Study a passage** adds *People and events* and *Confessions*: the articles and answers that cite the passage.
* **Back to my place, in Study mode,** now appears only just after a jump (a cross-reference, a search result, *Found N verses*) and goes once you read on (Books, previous or next chapter). It used to stay on every page whenever the service list had a current passage. Church mode is unchanged: the button shows whenever you are away from the list passage.
* **Saved lists:** *Delete* now really removes the list (deleting the list in use opens your most recent other list instead of putting an empty list of the same name in its place), and *New list* no longer adds a second empty list.

## Baiboly 1.5.0

**Install:** download the APK below and open it on the phone (allow installing from this source when asked).
Updates install over the app and keep your lists, notes, highlights and settings. From this version on, the app installs its own updates.

* **Study a passage, in every mode:** select verses, then *Study* (first on the selection bar). One panel, its tabs shown only when they have something for the passage:
  * *Translations*: the passage in every Bible offered, verse numbers matched between the Malagasy and the other texts; a tap reads it there.
  * *Original words* (א / α): the Hebrew or Greek words with their lexicon entries and *Found N verses*; a chip per verse when several are selected.
  * *Cross-references*; *Places* named, opened on the map; *Units* named (cubits, shekels, talents…), opened in the converter; *Dictionary*: Easton, Nave and Martin entries named by words of the passage, readable in place.
  * In Study mode a tap on a verse number still opens the same panel, on the original words.
* **More on the screen:** a *Compact* interface size (default; Settings › Appearance › Interface size) makes bars, buttons and lists smaller without changing the Bible text; narrower margins around the text and the verse numbers; text down to 10 (was 14); the selection actions on one row.
* **The screen stays on** while Baiboly is open (Settings › Appearance › Keep the screen on; on by default, always in Church mode).
* **Updates inside the app:** Settings › Advanced › Updates › *Download and install*.
  * The new version is downloaded in the app (with a progress bar and *Cancel*), checked against the SHA-256 published with the release, and handed to Android's installer, which asks you to confirm. Nothing opens in the browser.
  * The first time, Android asks you to allow Baiboly to install updates (*Allow from this source*); the app opens that page and continues when you come back.
  * The "new version" notification now opens these settings.
  * Still only on your tap, and nothing about you or your reading is sent. Updating *to* 1.5.0 is done the old way, from this page; the in-app update works from 1.5.0 to later versions.
* **The map fills the screen.** The controls float over it; *Show the bars* brings back the top and bottom bars. Zoom with + and −, a pinch or a double tap; *Fit the map* frames what is shown.
* **Search a place** by name (accents ignored), on the map's search field.
* **Regions drawn whole:** 192 kingdoms, peoples, provinces and natural regions outlined by OpenBible.info (CC BY 4.0): Edom, Moab, Ammon, Philistia, Galilee, Samaria, Judea, the Decapolis, Assyria, Egypt, Macedonia, Galatia, Asia…
  * Where the extent is uncertain, the region is shaded in bands: darker where it surely was, lighter towards an uncertain edge.
  * Tap inside a region, tap its name, or choose it under *Regions* (those of the chosen period). *Layers › Region outlines* draws them all faintly.
* **Routes:**
  * *Journeys*: 15 journeys of the Bible through the stops the text names, in order, with the reference of the account: Abraham, Jacob, Joseph, the Exodus (traditional route), the entry into Canaan, Elijah, the childhood of Jesus, his last journey to Jerusalem, Emmaus, Philip, Saul to Damascus, Paul's three journeys and the voyage to Rome. The line joins the stops; it is not the exact way taken.
  * *From… to…*: the way between any two places on the ancient roads (Roman roads and the Old Testament highways), with its length and the days on foot at about 30 km a day; a straight line when no road joins them (by sea). *Route from here* and *To here* on any place.
* **Units** (Study mode, Dictionary page, *Units*): length, weight, dry and liquid measures, money and time.
  * Type an amount, tap a unit: every other unit of the same kind and today's measure (metres, grams, litres; also feet, pounds and gallons in English). Money is also given in days' wages (a denarius a day, Mt 20:2).
  * Each unit has its name in the Malagasy 1865, Segond 1910 and King James texts, its Hebrew or Greek word, and the verse where it appears.
  * Relations stated by the text are marked "=" (20 gerahs = 1 shekel, Ex 30:13; 10 ephahs = 1 homer, Ezek 45:11…). Modern values are the usual estimates and are approximate.
  * The hours of the day (from sunrise) and the four watches of the night.
* **Also since 1.4.0** (from 1.4.1): the button that closes *What's new* reads *Close*; Settings no longer repeats the *Mode* choice (use the badge at the top left).
